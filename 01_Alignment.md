# Alignment

## What is Alignment?
Alignment is about creating the foundations for a product team to efficiently move through the rest of the phases.

It's crucial that everyone involved with the service have a clear understanding of the scope and approach to the work.

## What do you do in Alignment?

The main activity of Alignment is relationship building.

In Alignment you will:

- review the phases of Discovery, Alpha, Beta, Live
- determine who needs to be involved at a team, partnership, and executive level
- submit a [Privacy Impact Assessment](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/services-for-government/information-management-technology/privacy/privacy-impact-assessments?keyword=privacy&keyword=impact&keyword=assessment) to conduct research
- find a space for your product team to co-locate

## Staffing the Team

Alignment typically involves a small core group before the full delivery team is assembled:

| Role | Commitment | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| Service Owner / Executive Sponsor | Part-time | Controls budget; provides strategic direction; removes organisational blockers |
| Product Manager | Full-time | Leads day-to-day alignment activities; owns scope definition |
| Delivery Manager | Full-time | Facilitates process; coordinates across stakeholders |
| Subject Matter Expert | Part-time | Provides domain context and validates problem framing |
| Privacy / Legal Advisor | As needed | Reviews PIA requirements; identifies data governance constraints |

**For AI/agent projects**, also engage:
- **AI/ML Systems Thinker** (part-time): assesses technical feasibility and data availability early; flags model-dependency risks before the team commits to an AI-first approach

## What do you have at the end of Alignment?

By the end of Alignment, expect to have:

- a collective understanding of approach and expectations for Discovery, Alpha, Beta, Live
- an understanding who will be on the team for Discovery
- a co-located space for the team
- funding secured to support all phases
- budget allocated for research activities including stipends for research participants and travel
- formed any governance or oversight bodies that are required
- a completed [Privacy Impact Assessment](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/services-for-government/information-management-technology/privacy/privacy-impact-assessments?keyword=privacy&keyword=impact&keyword=assessment) for research activities

## Alignment Checklist

- [ ] Stakeholder map completed (team, partnership, and executive levels)
- [ ] All phases (Discovery, Alpha, Beta, Live) reviewed with key stakeholders
- [ ] Privacy Impact Assessment submitted
- [ ] Co-location space or remote collaboration tools established
- [ ] Funding confirmed for all subsequent phases
- [ ] Budget allocated for research activities (stipends, travel)
- [ ] Governance or oversight body formed (if required)
- [ ] Discovery team composition agreed
- [ ] **AI/agent projects:** data ethics and AI governance review initiated
- [ ] **AI/agent projects:** data access agreements and data ownership identified
- [ ] **AI/agent projects:** model vendor and infrastructure options reviewed at a high level

## AI/Agent Delivery Notes

For teams building AI agent systems, Alignment must additionally establish:

**Problem-fit assessment**: Is the problem well-suited to LLM/agent processing? Alignment is the right moment to do a high-level assessment — not a deep technical evaluation, but enough to confirm the approach is credible before investing in Discovery.

**Data ownership and provenance**: Who owns the data the AI will use? Are there licensing, privacy, or sovereignty constraints? Unresolved data agreements are the most common blocker encountered in Alpha — surface them now.

**Model vendor and infrastructure**: Which model providers are within policy? Are there air-gap requirements? Establish constraints early so Discovery teams are not scoping features around unavailable infrastructure.

**AI ethics governance**: Identify which AI ethics or algorithmic accountability frameworks apply. Some organisations require AI-specific impact assessments separate from the standard PIA.
